On the direct build, we may process your Google account email and Firebase user ID (Firebase Authentication) to create an account, restore unlock on a new device, and limit active devices.

We store a local unlock flag on your device after a successful purchase so the App can grant access offline. On store builds, Restore purchases recovers access via the same Google / Apple account. On the direct build, restore uses your Google account and our entitlement server.
